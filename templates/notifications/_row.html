The whole row is a link, which is right here and wrong on a post card: a notification has exactly one destination and nothing else to press, so there is no nested control for the anchor to swallow. Every branch renders the actor's name in bold as the subject of a sentence, which is why `notification_kind_targets` requires a non-null actor for both kinds rather than leaving this template to cope with a blank subject. NO `[deleted]` BRANCH, and that is not an oversight: anonymisation hard-deletes every notification where the departing user is the actor (ADR-0001, §8.1), so a row whose actor is a tombstone cannot exist.
