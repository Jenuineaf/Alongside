The primary navigation on mobile. {% block nav %}{% include "components/_tab_bar.html" with active="home" %}{% endblock %} Params: active home · explore · messages · alerts unread_messages / unread_alerts integers, badge hidden when 0 Five slots, and Post sits in the middle because posting an idea is the thing we most want to happen — it's the top of both loops in roadmap §1.3. Saved, Profile and Settings live behind the avatar in the top bar, not here; a six-item bar at 375px gives you 62px per target and labels that wrap. Hidden for signed-out visitors — they get a Sign up / Log in bar instead. Public browsing is non-negotiable (CONTRIBUTING.md rule 7), but a signed-out person has nothing to put in Messages or Alerts. The 11px label is a deliberate exception to the 12px type floor. At 375px, 12px labels wrap "Messages" onto two lines. The label is a redundant cue — the icon and aria-current carry the meaning — so shrinking it costs nothing that matters. Documented in design-system.md §2. {% endcomment %}
