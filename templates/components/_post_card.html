The Message shortcut on the card. READING ORDER — this is the design, not an accident who → what → how far along → what they need → what it's about → where → what you can do. A joiner scanning the feed is looking for one thing: a role they can fill. Roles sit above the tags and below the stage badges so that by the time you reach them you already know whether the commitment level is worth your time. The title is the only link to the detail page. Do NOT wrap the whole card in an anchor: it swallows the Spark button, the author link and the tags, and it makes the card unusable with a keyboard. {% endcomment %}
