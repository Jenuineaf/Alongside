{% comment %} Empty state — OWNED BY THE DESIGNER. {% include "components/_empty_state.html" with icon="messages" title="No conversations yet" body="Find an idea you'd want to help with and say hello. Most people reply — nobody posts here hoping to be left alone." action_label="Browse ideas" action_url="/feed/" %} Params: icon · title · body · action_label + action_url (optional) Empty states are not filler. For a brand-new user they ARE the product — the cold-start problem is won or lost here, and an empty screen that says "No results" is how a portfolio demo dies in the first ten seconds. Three rules, learned from every feed product that got this wrong: 1. Say WHY it's empty, not just that it is. 2. Give exactly one way out, as a real button. 3. Never blame the user. "No ideas match those filters" — not "You have no results". Filters cause this far more often than the words do. For the Feed and Home specifically, DON'T use this: the cascading fallback (product-spec §4.3) means those are never empty. They get a labelled divider explaining where the posts came from instead. {% endcomment %}
