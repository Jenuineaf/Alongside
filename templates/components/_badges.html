They are the reason someone doesn't waste three weeks on a "serious startup" that was a weekend doodle. So they get real badges on the card, not muted meta text. WHY STAGE GETS A METER Stage is ordinal — just an idea → already building → launched. A 3-segment meter says "1 of 3" before you read the word, which is information the flat dot in the first mockup was throwing away. Commitment is binary, so it gets a dot: hollow for casual, filled brand for serious. COLOUR IS NEVER THE ONLY SIGNAL Every badge carries its word. Status also carries a dot, but the word is what does the work — this survives greyscale, and with a green brand and red/green colour blindness that matters more here than on a blue app. {% endcomment %}
