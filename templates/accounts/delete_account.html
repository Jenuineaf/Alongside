Delete your account

You have 30 days to change your mind. Nothing is removed until then, and logging back in at any point cancels it.

After 30 days we permanently remove your name, photo, bio, links, interests and skills, and your email address. Your login stops working.

Your ideas, comments and messages stay up, shown as [deleted]. They're part of other people's threads — a conversation shouldn't become a monologue because one person left.
