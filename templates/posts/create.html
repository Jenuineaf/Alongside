There are no drafts in v1 (§2.3.1) — a post is live when created. The Designer drew one and removed it in the v6 sweep (GAP 6), flagging that if it had shipped, somebody would have built a draft state to satisfy the button. Two fields the schema has and this form deliberately does not capture: `PostRole.note` (GAP 5) and `PostImage.caption` (GAP 7), both open Designer questions. Image upload is absent too — it is first on the roadmap's cut list.
