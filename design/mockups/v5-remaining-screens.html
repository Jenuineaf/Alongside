4 · Home for a brand-new user — the cold start

The most important screen in the product and the one that was missing. A new account has almost no graph, so the cascading fallback (§4.3) does the work — and the design job is saying out loud where each block came from. An unexplained feed feels random; a labelled one feels like the app is working on your behalf. Each divider is also a nudge toward the action that would improve it.

What Alongside is

Danger zone

Deleting your account removes your profile and messages. Your ideas stay up, credited to "a former member" — so the comment threads other people built don't vanish with you.

Check this one: the deletion copy reflects ADR-0001 (anonymise, don't cascade). If that ADR says something different from what I've written, the ADR wins and I'll fix the copy.

What I need from you

  1. 1 · The new-user Home (§4). The most consequential screen here. I've made each band label its own source — "from people you follow", "because you picked android". Does that feel helpful or does it feel like the app apologising?
  2. 2 · Follow-with-a-note (§7). This had no design and I've invented one: the follow happens immediately, then the note is offered as an optional extra. The alternative is asking for the note first, which adds friction to a one-tap action. I think follow-first is right — confirm?
  3. 3 · The "About" context line in the chat thread (§10). Showing which idea a conversation came from isn't in the spec. I think it's necessary, since anyone can DM anyone and "why is this person messaging me" is the first question every time. It's a small ask of the Messaging seat.
  4. 4 · Delete-account copy (§13). I've written it to match ADR-0001. Worth a check that "credited to a former member" is the actual behaviour.

Once you're happy, the feature seats have everything: docs/design-system.md for the rules, templates/components/ for the parts, and these screens for the layouts.
