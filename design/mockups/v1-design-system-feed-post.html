1 · Colour

Every competitor in this space is blue or purple. Alongside is warm — it has to feel as natural to a crochet-business owner as to a developer. The brand ramp is an ember, which is also literally what Spark is. Rule: one loud colour per card — the spark. Everything else is quiet, or a dense feed becomes a fruit salad.

2 · Type

No web font. The system stack renders Roboto on Android and Segoe on Windows — zero download, zero layout shift, instant first paint on a mid-range phone over 4G. Brand personality lives in the wordmark (SVG) and the colour, not in a 90KB font file. This is a deliberate performance call for our users, and a good one to be able to defend.

3 · Spacing, shape, elevation

Spacing — 4px base

Only 1 2 3 4 6 8 12 (4→48px). Card padding 16 mobile / 20 desktop. Screen gutter 16. Gap between cards 8.

Radius

chips full · inputs & buttons lg 8 · cards & images 2xl 16 · avatars full.

Elevation

Borders, not shadows. One shadow token, for things that float over the page only (sheets, dropdowns, toasts). Flat renders faster and looks less dated.

Touch targets: 44×44 minimum, always. Icon buttons are 40px visually with padding to 44.

6 · How it scales up (≥1024px)

Same components, more room. The bottom bar becomes a left rail, filters move out of the sheet into a persistent right rail. The centre column stays at 640px — a post is text, and text past ~70 characters per line is harder to read, not more impressive.

7 · Four things I need you to decide

  1. Warm ember, or something cooler? Everything else follows from this. I chose warm because the crochet-business owner has to feel as at home here as the developer — and because Spark is an ember.
  2. Home and Feed as two tabs in one shell (my proposal) or two separate destinations in the nav? Tabs are fewer taps and one HTMX swap; separate destinations are more explicit.
  3. "Message" on the feed card — I've drawn it in because it shortcuts your core loop, but it does add weight to every card. Cut it and messaging starts from the post detail and profile only.
  4. Dark mode. A lot of your users are on Android with dark mode on all day. Doing it later is a retrofit; doing it now roughly doubles the visual review. My recommendation: build light-only for v1 but write every colour as a semantic token, so dark mode is a token swap in Phase 2 rather than a rewrite.
